Seybakh Studios

Seybakh Studios makes games and digital experiences. It is the games branch of Seybakh, and the studio behind Evolve: Solar Age.

Evolve: Solar Age

Evolve: Solar Age is an idle incremental game where you ignite the Sun, develop Earth from a lifeless world into life and civilization, collect resources, unlock upgrades, and expand through the solar system at a calm, steady pace.

Read about Evolve: Solar Age

How the work is made

One project at a time, finished before the next one starts. The aim is a game with a character of its own: a calm pace, systems that reward attention, and a look that belongs to Seybakh rather than to a template.

What comes next

More work will be shown here when it is ready to be seen. Nothing else is announced, and nothing carries a date.
