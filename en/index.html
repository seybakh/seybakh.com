The story

Seybakh began as one creator identity: one person, one name, a habit of finishing things. The ideas outgrew a single label, so the name widened instead of splitting. Studios took the games and digital experiences. The Channel took the video.

Where it is going

A wider world under one name: games with their own voice, video worth the time it asks for, and products and services if they earn their place. Each branch opens when its work can stand alone.

What is active now

Two branches are active: Seybakh Studios and Seybakh Channel. Merch and Services are named because they are next, not because they are open — nothing there is for sale.
